My Favourite Anime

My favourite anime is Mushoku Tensei: Jobless Reincarnation. It is about a 34-year-old man who dies in an accident and is reborn as a baby in a world of magic and swords. He keeps all of his old memories, so this time he gets a second chance to live his life properly.

I like this show because the story takes its time and you really get to watch the characters grow up. The magic looks beautiful too, and the whole show is painted in blue and gold, which is where I got the colours for this blog.
